---
title: Booking equipment online
summary: For renters: check availability, book, pay and sign from your phone.
order: 4
related: for-customers/rental-questions
---

1. On your yard's booking page, choose the equipment you need.
2. Pick your **Pick up** and **Return** days. Only days when a unit is free can be booked. Equipment is ready at opening time and due back by closing time.
3. Choose whether to add the damage waiver.
4. Enter your name, email and mobile phone (for pickup texts).
5. Select **Book and pay** and pay by card. Your card is saved for the deposit.
6. Read and sign the rental agreement on your phone.

You'll get a confirmation, and a copy of the signed agreement by email. Bring your driver's licence to pickup.
