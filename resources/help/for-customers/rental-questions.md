### When is my deposit released?

When the equipment comes back and is checked in. The deposit is a hold on your card, not a charge. Any extra charges are taken from it and the rest is released. Your bank may take a few days to show the hold has gone.

### What if I bring it back late?

There's a short grace period. After that, the extra time is charged at the yard's rates. The yard's late fee policy is on your agreement.

### Do I need to refuel or clean it?

Bring it back full and clean to avoid fuel and cleaning charges.

### What if something gets damaged?

Tell the yard right away. Damage is checked against the photos taken when the equipment went out. If you took the damage waiver, your agreement says what it covers.

### Will I get reminders?

If the yard turns them on, you'll get a text the day before pickup, before it's due back, and if it's overdue.
