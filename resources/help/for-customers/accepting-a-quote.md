---
title: Accepting a quote online
summary: For customers: review a quote from your yard and accept it.
order: 3
related: for-customers/contractor-portal
---

When a yard sends you a quote, you get an email with a link to it.

1. Open the link to see the items, quantities, prices, delivery, tax, any refundable deposits and the total.
2. Enter **Your name** and, if your company uses them, a **PO number**.
3. Select **Accept quote**.

The yard is told you've accepted and will confirm the order with you. Prices hold until the date on the quote. After that, call the yard for an updated price.
