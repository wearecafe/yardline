Owners and admins review time under **Team**.

## Who's in

**Who's in** shows everyone who clocks in, by yard: working, on a break or out, where they punched, hours today and this week, and anyone getting close to overtime (within four hours of it). Leave it open on the office screen; it refreshes every minute.

## Timesheets

**Timesheets** shows the pay period by person, with anything that needs a look at the top:

| Flag                       | What happened                                                         |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No clock-out**           | Clocked in and never out. That shift counts no hours until it's fixed |
| **Punch with no clock-in** | A clock-out or break with nothing before it                           |
| **Long shift**             | Longer than the limit in **Settings → Time**                          |
| **No meal break**          | Worked longer than the meal limit in a row without a meal break       |
| **Short rest**             | Back in less than the daily rest after the last shift                 |
| **Away from the yard**     | Punched from a phone away from every yard                             |
| **No location**            | Punched from a phone with location turned off                         |
| **Sent late from phone**   | Punched with no signal and sent later                                 |
| **Disputed edit**          | They disagree with a change you made                                  |

Open someone to see each day's punches.

## Fixing time

Every fix needs a reason. The original punch is kept, marked as changed, and the person gets an email and sees the change on their timesheet.

- **Change** or **Remove** a punch.
- **Add a missed punch** to a day, such as the clock-out someone forgot.
- **Adjust hours** to add (or, with a minus sign, take off) time on a day without a punch, as regular time or overtime.

A change to a submitted or approved timesheet sends it back for approval. So does a new punch on an approved one.

## Disputes

When someone disputes a change, it shows on their day. Talk it over, fix the time if they're right, then tap **Mark sorted out**.

## Approving

**Approve** freezes someone's hours for payroll. It waits until there's no missing clock-out, no open dispute and they're off the clock. **Approve all that are ready** approves everyone else in one go and tells you who's left.

Every change, approval and lock is in the [audit log](/help/getting-started/audit-log).
