Time and attendance is an add-on, billed per person who clocks in. An owner or admin turns it on under **Settings → Time** by ticking **Track time for this business**.

The first time it's turned on, everyone who signs in to YardRunner is set up to clock in: staff, admins and drivers hourly, and the owner salaried and not clocking in. Change any of that under **Team → Employees**.

## Who clocks in

**Team → Employees** lists everyone whose hours YardRunner keeps.

| Setting                     | What it does                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Signs in to YardRunner as** | Links them to a sign-in so they can clock in from their own phone. Leave as **Time clock only** for a yard hand who just uses the counter tablet |
| **PIN for the time clock**  | 4 to 6 digits, entered at the counter tablet. Anyone without a PIN can't use the tablet                                                          |
| **Pay**                     | Hourly or salaried. Salaried people don't earn overtime                                                                                          |
| **Payroll ID**              | Their number in your payroll system (the ADP file number)                                                                                        |
| **Clocks in**               | Turn off for staff who only take time off                                                                                                        |
| **No overtime**             | For managers and other roles that don't earn overtime                                                                                            |
| **Takes a truck home**      | Lets a driver start and end the day away from the yard without a flag                                                                            |

Pay rates aren't kept in YardRunner; they stay in your payroll system.

When someone leaves, **Archive** them. Their past hours stay on file and still go to payroll for the periods they worked.

## Where each yard is

Phone punches are checked against your yards. Under **Settings → Time → Time clock and phones**, enter each yard's latitude and longitude (right-click the yard gate in Google Maps to copy them) and how far out still counts as the yard.

A punch away from every yard is still recorded. It's just flagged for a manager to look at, because drivers and site crews punch from job sites for good reasons.

> **Note** Location is only taken at the moment someone punches. YardRunner doesn't follow anyone while they're clocked out. If you have 25 or more employees in Ontario, your written electronic monitoring policy should mention it.

## Photos at the time clock

**Photo on time clock punches** takes a picture with the tablet's front camera on each punch: for everyone, or for the people you pick under **Team → Employees**. It's off to start with.
