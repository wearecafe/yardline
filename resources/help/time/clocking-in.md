There are three ways to punch, and they all go to the same timesheet.

## At the counter tablet

Open **Team → Time clock** on the tablet at the counter or in the yard shack. It stays on the list of names; nobody needs to sign in for each punch.

1. Tap your name.
2. Tap **Clock in**, **Start break**, **End break** or **Clock out**.
3. Enter your PIN and tap **OK**.

The screen shows the time you punched and your hours so far this week, then goes back to the names. If the tablet has more than one yard, pick the yard it's at from the top of the screen.

> **Heads up** Five wrong PINs in a row lock that person out of the tablet for five minutes.

## On your phone

Open **Team → My time** (drivers: tap **Clock in** at the top of the driver app). The big button changes with where you are in your day. Allow location when your phone asks: it's checked against the yard once, when you punch.

No signal? Punch anyway. The punch waits on your phone with the time you made it and goes as soon as you're back online. The office sees it was sent late.

## Forgot to clock out?

Clock in as usual the next day. YardRunner starts a new shift and flags the one you didn't finish, and your manager adds the time you left.

## Your timesheet

**My time** shows this pay period's days, your regular and overtime hours, and any change a manager made, with their reason. If a change isn't right, tap **This isn't right** under it and say what it should be; it goes back to your manager before payroll. When your period looks right, tap **Send this timesheet for approval**.
