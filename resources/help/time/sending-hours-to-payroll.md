Pay periods are set under **Settings → Time → Pay periods**: weekly, every two weeks from a start date, or twice a month (the 1st to the 15th and the 16th to the end).

## Lock the period

Once a period has ended and everyone's timesheet is approved, tap **Lock for payroll** on **Team → Timesheets**. Nothing in a locked period can change. **Reopen** unlocks it if you find a mistake before exporting.

## Export

| Export                  | What you get                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hours summary (CSV)** | Each person's regular, overtime and total hours, with their payroll ID, for any payroll service or your accountant                                                                 |
| **ADP import (CSV)**    | Company code, batch, file number, regular and overtime hours. Add your **Company code** under **Settings → Time** and everyone's **Payroll ID** first                              |
| **Send to QuickBooks**  | A time activity for each person for each day, regular and overtime apart, on the matching QuickBooks employee (added if they aren't there yet). Shown once QuickBooks is connected |

Hours are the totals from when each timesheet was approved.

> **Tip** Check the ADP layout with your ADP rep the first time; some ADP products expect different columns.

## After export

An exported period never changes and can't be reopened. If you find a mistake later, open the person's timesheet in the current period and use **Adjust hours** on today with a reason that says which day it's for.
