One set of rules covers everyone in the business. Set them under **Settings → Time → Overtime and breaks**; choosing a province fills in its usual standards.

| Rule                                   | Ontario  | Quebec   |
| -------------------------------------- | -------- | -------- |
| Overtime after, each workweek          | 44 hours | 40 hours |
| Meal break due after, in a row         | 5 hours  | 5 hours  |
| Meal break                             | 30 min   | 30 min   |
| Flag less than this off between shifts | 11 hours | Off      |

These are starting points from each province's employment standards. Check them against the current rules, and against any overtime averaging agreement you have.

## How hours add up

- A shift belongs to the day it starts, even if it runs past midnight.
- Breaks are unpaid: time between **Start break** and **End break** is taken off.
- Overtime counts over the workweek (it starts on the day you pick), at time and a half. A pay period that starts mid-week still counts the earlier days of that week toward overtime.
- Salaried people and anyone marked **No overtime** get all their hours as regular time.

## Rounding

**Round punches** is off to start with. If you turn it on, every clock in and out rounds to the nearest step, up or down evenly, so it never shortchanges anyone.

## Automatic meal breaks

**Take the meal break off automatically** deducts the meal break from a shift longer than the meal limit when nobody punched a break. It's off to start with; it's better for people to punch their breaks.
