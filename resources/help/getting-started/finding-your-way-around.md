The sidebar groups YardRunner into three sections.

## Supply yard

| Screen             | Use it to                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Today              | See the latest orders and what's running low, and start a new order or rental |
| Counter            | Ring up a sale and take payment on the spot                                   |
| Orders and picking | Work the pick queue and open any order                                        |
| Dispatch           | Put deliveries on trucks and send ETA texts                                   |
| Products           | Add and edit materials and rental equipment                                   |
| Stock              | See stock by yard, bin and colour run, and receive, count, adjust or move it  |
| Purchasing         | Reorder, raise purchase orders and receive deliveries                         |
| Returns            | Take returns, issue credit notes and credit pallets                           |
| Yards              | Manage your locations                                                         |

## Rental

| Screen                | Use it to                                          |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Fleet                 | See every unit, its status, hours and next service |
| Calendar              | See availability by unit and move bookings         |
| Rentals and contracts | Quote, reserve, sign, check out and check in       |
| Maintenance           | Log service and repairs, and see what's due        |
| Rental payments       | See payments, deposits on hold and what's owing    |

## Accounts

| Screen        | Use it to                                                             |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organizations | Manage contractor and builder accounts, job sites and contract prices |
| Customers     | Manage people, including walk-ins and renters                         |
| Receivables   | See aging, statements and credits                                     |
| Price lists   | Manage trade pricing                                                  |

**Settings** (from your name at the bottom of the sidebar) has your profile and security, [alerts and installing the app](/help/getting-started/app-and-alerts), and for owners and admins, the business, delivery, rental, QuickBooks and audit log pages.
