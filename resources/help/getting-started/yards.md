A yard is any location that keeps its own stock. Every order is filled from one yard, and stock, bins, counts and receiving are all tracked per yard.

## Add a yard

1. Open **Yards** in the sidebar and select **Add a yard**.
2. Enter a name and a short code (for example `MAIN` or `NORTH`).
3. Enter the street address, city, province and postal code. Pickup orders are taxed at this address.
4. Tick **Default yard for new orders** for the yard most orders come from.

## How yards are used

- **Orders** pick a yard. New orders start at your default yard, and a job site can have its own usual yard.
- **Stock** shows on-hand, committed and available by yard. See [Stock levels](/help/inventory/stock-overview).
- **Transfers** move stock between yards. See [Transferring stock](/help/inventory/transferring-stock).
- **Rental units** each have a home yard.
