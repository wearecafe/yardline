Yardline runs a masonry, landscape or building supply yard: what you sell and rent, who you sell it to, at what price, and how it gets out the gate. Everything happens in a browser, so the counter can use a desktop or tablet, the yard a rugged tablet, and drivers their phones.

## The main parts

| Part                 | What it covers                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Counter and orders   | Walk-in sales, orders for accounts, quotes, and confirming on account or prepaid             |
| Products and pricing | Selling units (bag, pallet, tonne, cu yd…), price lists, quantity breaks and contract prices |
| Accounts             | Organizations, their contacts and job sites, credit limits, terms and holds                  |
| Yard and delivery    | The pick queue, the yard kiosk, trucks, the dispatch board, ETA texts and proof of delivery  |
| Stock and purchasing | Stock by yard, bin and colour run, purchase orders, receiving, counts and transfers          |
| Money                | Sales tax, payments, invoices, aging, statements, returns, credit notes and pallet deposits  |
| Equipment rentals    | Your fleet, rates, availability, agreements, deposits, inspections and maintenance           |
| Your customers       | A contractor portal and a public booking page for rentals                                    |

## How an order moves

1. An order starts as a **draft**: at the counter, from a quote, or as a reorder from the contractor portal.
2. **Confirming** it calculates tax, decides whether it goes on the account or must be paid up front, and issues the invoice.
3. The yard **picks** it on the kiosk and **loads** it. Stock comes off as it leaves.
4. Deliveries go on the **dispatch board**, customers get an ETA text, and the driver takes a **signature and photo** at the drop.

Rentals follow their own path, from quote to reservation, signed agreement, check-out and check-in. See [How rentals work](/help/rentals/how-rentals-work).

## One system for the whole yard

Yardline is the system of record for your products, prices, accounts and orders, and it's your point of sale too. Counter sales, orders your staff enter and orders from the contractor portal all land in the same place. Your books stay in QuickBooks Online. See [QuickBooks Online and your accounting](/help/integrations/quickbooks).
