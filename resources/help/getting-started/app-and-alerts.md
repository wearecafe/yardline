YardRunner runs in a web browser. You can also add it to the home screen of a phone or tablet, or install it on a computer, so it opens from its own icon. This is done from your browser: YardRunner isn't in the App Store or Google Play, and there's nothing to download.

## What installing does

- YardRunner opens full screen from its own icon, without the browser's address bar.
- It opens straight to the home screen for your roles, so the same install works on the counter tablet, the yard tablet and a driver's phone.
- On iPhone and iPad, it's the only way to get [alerts](#turn-on-alerts).

It's the same YardRunner as in the browser, with the same screens. It still needs a signal to work (see [No connection](#no-connection)).

## Add it to your home screen

The steps depend on the device. **Settings → Alerts → Install the app** shows the steps for the device you're on.

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open YardRunner in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open YardRunner from the new icon on your home screen and sign in again.

### Android

1. Open YardRunner in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Alerts** and select **Install YardRunner**. If there's no button, tap the **⋮** menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open YardRunner from the new icon.

### Computer

In **Chrome** or **Edge**, select the install icon at the end of the address bar, or **Install YardRunner** under **Settings → Alerts**. YardRunner then opens in its own window.

### Updates

There's nothing to update by hand. When a new version is out, a message says **A new version of YardRunner is ready**. Select **Reload** when you're at a good stopping point.

## Turn on alerts

Alerts pop up on the device for new work, even when YardRunner is closed. They're set up per device: turn them on on each phone or tablet you want them on.

1. Open **Settings → Alerts** on the device.
2. Select **Turn on alerts**, then allow notifications when the browser asks.
3. Select **Send a test alert** to check it works.

People whose roles get alerts also see a one-time **Get alerts on this device** prompt at the top of the screen. Select **Turn on alerts** there, or close it and turn them on from Settings later. On iPhone and iPad the prompt asks you to add YardRunner to your home screen first.

> **Heads up** On iPhone and iPad, alerts only work from the installed app. Add YardRunner to the home screen first, open it from there, then turn alerts on.

## What you get alerts for

What you get depends on your roles and yards. **Settings → Alerts** lists yours under **You get an alert when**.

| Who                              | Alert                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Yard crew                        | A new order lands in the pick queue at one of their yards                                               |
| Yard crew                        | A quality check on their work is rejected, with the reason                                              |
| Anyone who can sign off a check  | A load is waiting for a quality check at **Picked** or **Loaded** (not sent to whoever marked it)       |
| Drivers                          | They're put on a truck for today or a later day                                                         |
| Drivers                          | A stop is added to today's run after the day has started                                                |
| Rental desk                      | A rental at one of their yards is overdue, once a day while it's late (with the Rentals module turned on) |

Tapping an alert opens the right screen: the pick queue, the order, the driver's run or the rental. Alerts never show prices. Counter sales that are picked up on the spot don't send a pick alert.

## Shared tablets and your devices

**Your devices** on **Settings → Alerts** lists every device that gets your alerts and when it last got one. Select **Remove** to stop alerts on a device you no longer use, or **Turn off here** on the device itself.

Alerts follow whoever is signed in. When someone else signs in on a shared tablet, its alerts move to them.

## No connection

If a phone or tablet loses signal, a strip along the bottom says **No connection** and changes won't save until it's back. Opening a screen with no signal shows a **No connection** page instead. Nothing already saved is lost, and the page reloads by itself when the signal comes back. YardRunner doesn't work without a signal, so wait for one before picking, delivering or taking payment.

## If alerts won't turn on

| What you see                                        | What to do                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Alerts only work from YardRunner on your home screen | On iPhone or iPad, install the app first (see above), then open it from there    |
| Alerts are blocked in this browser's settings       | Allow notifications for YardRunner in the browser's site settings, then reload  |
| This browser can't show alerts                      | Use Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari                                              |
| Alerts are off in the demo yard                     | The demo is shared by everyone trying it, so it doesn't send alerts              |
