Everyone signs in with their own account, so sales, overrides and changes are recorded against their name in the [audit log](/help/getting-started/audit-log).

## Roles

| Role   | Can do                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner  | Everything. The person who created the business is its owner.                                                                                                                                             |
| Admin  | Everything an owner can, including settings, line price changes, credit limits, terms and holds, and credit overrides.                                                                                    |
| Staff  | Sell, quote, take payments, pick, load, deliver, receive stock, and run rentals. Can't change settings, change a price on an order line, change an account's credit settings, or override a credit check. |
| Driver | See their own truck's run on their phone, mark stops on their way or done, and take proof of delivery. Nothing else. See [The driver app](/help/yard-delivery/driver-app).                                |

## Adding staff

Owners and admins add drivers themselves under **Settings → Drivers**. See [Setting up drivers](/help/yard-delivery/setting-up-drivers).

Inviting other staff from inside Yardline is coming soon. Until then, contact us and we'll add staff accounts to your business.

## Signing in on shared devices

Yard kiosks and counter tablets are often shared. Sign out at the end of a shift (from your name at the bottom of the sidebar) so the next person's work is recorded under their own name.
