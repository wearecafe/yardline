When you create your account, YardRunner walks you through **Setup**. Two steps are required before anything else opens; the rest you can skip and come back to.

## 1. Create your account

Go to **Set up your yard** on the YardRunner home page and enter your business name, your name and your email. You become the business's **owner**, with access to every setting. Confirm your email from the message we send, and Setup starts.

## 2. Your business (required)

Check your business name, the email customer replies should go to, and your time zone. Add your tax numbers (for example your HST number) so they're printed on invoices and receipts. You can change all of this later under **Settings → Business** and **Settings → Branding**.

## 3. Your yards (required)

Enter every yard you keep stock and sell from, with its full address. Pickup orders are taxed at the yard's address, and delivery distances are measured from it. Once your yards are saved, YardRunner is open. See [Adding yards](/help/getting-started/yards).

## 4. The optional steps

Setup then asks:

- **How you work**: whether you deliver with your own trucks, sell to contractors on account, rent equipment or track staff hours. Renting and tracking hours switch on the Rentals and Time & attendance add-ons, free for the rest of your trial.
- **Your products**: add your first products, or send us your price list and we'll load it.
- **Your team**: invite people with the role for their job. They get an email to choose a password. Add drivers under **Settings → Drivers** once your trucks are in. See [Staff and roles](/help/getting-started/staff-and-roles).
- **Logo and colour**: for quotes, invoices and every customer email.
- **Card payments and tax**: set up card payments and your sales tax registrations under **Settings → Payments**.

Skip any of these and they stay on the **Finish setting up** checklist on **Today**, which ticks itself off as you do each one, wherever you do it. Hide it once you don't need it.

> **Note** Only owners and admins go through Setup. People you invite go straight to their own screens.

The rest of this guide covers what to do once you're in.

## 5. Set delivery, returns and deposits

Under **Settings → Delivery**, choose how you charge for delivery, add your trucks, and set your restocking fee and pallet deposit. See [Delivery fees](/help/yard-delivery/delivery-fees) and [Pallet and container deposits](/help/money/pallet-deposits).

## 6. Add your products

Add what you sell, with a base unit and the selling units you sell it in. See [Adding products](/help/products-pricing/add-a-product) and [Selling units](/help/products-pricing/selling-units).

## 7. Set up trade pricing

Create price lists (for example Contractor and Builder) and give them to your accounts. See [Price lists](/help/products-pricing/price-lists).

## 8. Enter opening stock

On **Stock**, use **Update stock → Receive** or a cycle count to put your current quantities in each yard. See [Adjusting stock](/help/inventory/adjusting-stock).

## 9. Add your accounts

Add your contractor and builder accounts as organizations, with their contacts, job sites, terms and credit limits. See [Organizations](/help/accounts/organizations).

## 10. Add your rental fleet

If you rent equipment, see [Rental equipment and rates](/help/rentals/rental-equipment-and-rates) and [Adding units to the fleet](/help/rentals/fleet-units).

> **Tip** Ring up a test sale and a test rental before you open. It's the quickest way to check tax, deposits and receipts.
