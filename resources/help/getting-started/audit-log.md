YardRunner records changes to orders, prices and credit terms with the person who made them and the values before and after.

## Where to find it

- **Settings → Audit log** lists every change across the business. Search by order number, product or account.
- **Order and organization pages** have a **History** section showing changes to that record.

Changes made by contractors through the [contractor portal](/help/for-customers/contractor-portal), like a reorder, are recorded under the contact's name.

> **Note** **Settings → Audit log** is only available to owners and admins.
