Owners and admins add drivers. Each driver gets their own sign-in, so proof of delivery and location are recorded against the right person.

## Add a driver

1. Go to **Settings → Drivers**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and pick the truck they **Drive**.
3. Select **Add driver**.

They get an email to choose a password. The link works for 60 minutes; select **Send link again** if it runs out. Once they've chosen a password the driver shows **Signed in**.

## Put a driver on a truck

Each truck has one driver who signs in. Under **Settings → Delivery → Trucks**, pick the driver in **Driver's sign-in** and select **Save**. To swap drivers for the day, change it here; the run moves with the truck.

Trucks can still have a **Driver** name typed in for drivers who don't sign in. The dispatch board shows the signed-in driver's name when there is one.

## Remove a driver

Select **Remove** next to them under **Settings → Drivers**. They're signed out straight away and their truck goes back to having no sign-in.

> **Note** Office and yard staff can open any truck's run at `/driver` and choose the truck at the top. It's handy for checking what a driver sees.
