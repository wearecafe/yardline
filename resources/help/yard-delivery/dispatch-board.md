The dispatch board replaces the whiteboard. Each truck is a row and each hour of the day is a column.

## Set up your trucks

Under **Settings → Delivery → Trucks**, add each truck with:

- A name, for example "Truck 1 · tandem" or "Boom 1"
- What it **Carries**: material, rental equipment, or both
- **Capacity (t)** and whether it has a **Boom**
- The usual **Driver**, and their **Driver's sign-in** if they use [the driver app](/help/yard-delivery/driver-app)
- **On the board**: turn off a truck that's in for repairs

## Schedule the day

Open **Dispatch** and choose the day. Unscheduled stops are listed first:

- **Material deliveries** once they're loaded
- **Rental drop-offs and pickups** for delivered rentals
- **Pallet pickups** requested by contractors from the portal

Drag a stop onto a truck at the hour it should go. It appears on that driver's phone straight away. Each truck shows its driver and capacity, and when it was last seen (see [Where the trucks are](/help/yard-delivery/truck-locations)), so put orders marked **Needs a boom truck** on a boom. Drag a stop to move it, or select **Unschedule** to take it off.

## ETA texts

Select **Send ETA texts** to text every scheduled customer their delivery window, a two-hour window from the stop's time. Each customer gets one text, and the stop shows **ETA text sent**. Turn this off under **Settings → Delivery** with **Text customers their delivery window**.

## On the road

When a truck leaves, the driver selects **On my way** on their phone (or mark the stop **On its way** here). At the drop, the driver records [proof of delivery](/help/yard-delivery/proof-of-delivery).
