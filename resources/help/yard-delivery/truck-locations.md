When a driver has their run open, their phone shares the truck's position. You see it in two places, and you decide whether contractors see anything.

## On the dispatch board

Under each truck's name, **Seen 3 min ago** shows when its position last came in. It's green while it's recent and grey after 15 minutes. Select it to open the spot in a map.

No position? The driver hasn't opened their run today, their phone's location is off, or the truck has no driver who signs in.

## In the contractor portal

Under **Settings → Delivery**, in **In the contractor portal**, you can turn on either or both:

- **Show how many stops are before theirs**: while a delivery is on a truck today, the contractor's order page shows "2 stops before yours", or "Your delivery is next".
- **Show the truck on a map when theirs is next**: once their delivery is the next stop and the driver has selected **On my way**, the order page shows the truck on a map, updated every minute.

Both are off until you turn them on.

> **Note** The truck only shows on a contractor's map while it's on its way to them. While it's heading to another customer, they see the stop count only, so nobody can follow the truck to someone else's site.

## Privacy

Location is shared only while a driver has their run open, never in the background. Positions are kept for 30 days and then deleted. Let your drivers know their location is shared while they're working their run.
