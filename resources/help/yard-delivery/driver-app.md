Drivers sign in to YardRunner on their own phone and see one thing: the run for the truck they drive, in order. There's nothing to install. It runs in the phone's browser.

> **Tip** Add the page to the home screen (**Share → Add to Home Screen** on iPhone, **⋮ → Add to Home screen** on Android) so the run is one tap away.

## The run

**My run** lists the day's stops for the driver's truck by time. Each stop shows what it is (delivery, rental drop-off or pickup, pallet pickup), the customer, the address and where it stands:

| Status         | Means                                              |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Scheduled      | On the truck's run, not left yet                   |
| Not loaded yet | The order is still in the yard. Check before going |
| On its way     | The driver has left, with the ETA they gave        |
| Delivered/Done | Finished, with the time                            |

The next stop is outlined, with **Directions** (opens the phone's maps app), **Call** (the site contact) and **Proof of delivery** right on it. Use the arrows at the top to look at yesterday or tomorrow.

## At each stop

Tap a stop to see everything for it: what's on the truck for that stop, the PO number, site notes (for example "Gate code 4412"), order notes, and whether it **Needs a boom truck**.

1. When you leave for it, check **Arriving about** and select **On my way**. Dispatch and the customer's portal see it's on its way.
2. At a material delivery, select **Proof of delivery**, then take the name, a signature and a photo of the drop. See [Proof of delivery and pickup](/help/yard-delivery/proof-of-delivery).
3. Rental drops and pickups and pallet pickups finish with **Dropped off**, **Picked up** or **Pallets collected**.

## Sharing location

While the run is open on the phone, it shares the truck's position with dispatch about once a minute, and straight away when the truck has moved. The header shows **Sharing location**. The first time, the phone asks for permission: choose **Allow**.

> **Heads up** Phones only share location while the page is on screen. If the phone is locked or another app is in front, sharing pauses and picks up again when the run is opened. Navigation apps on the same phone are fine: switch back to YardRunner between stops.

If the header says **Location off**, location was refused for the site. Turn it back on in the phone's browser settings for this site.

Positions are kept for 30 days and are only seen by your yard, and by contractors if you turn that on. See [Where the trucks are](/help/yard-delivery/truck-locations).

## What drivers can't see

Drivers see only their own truck's run. They can't open orders, prices, accounts, receivables, other trucks' runs or settings, apart from their own profile and password.
