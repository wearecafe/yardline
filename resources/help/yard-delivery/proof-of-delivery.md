Every load that leaves the yard is signed for, whether it's delivered or picked up at the yard.

## At the drop

The driver selects **Proof of delivery** on the stop in [the driver app](/help/yard-delivery/driver-app) (or the office opens it from the dispatch board), then:

1. Enters who it was **Received by**.
2. Gets a **Signature** on the screen.
3. Takes a **Photo of the drop**, especially when nobody's there to sign.
4. Adds **Notes**, for example "Left by the garage".
5. Selects **Delivered**.

The stop shows as delivered on the driver's run, the dispatch board and the contractor's portal.

## At the yard

When a pickup is ready, select **Hand over** on it in **Orders and picking** (or on the order's shipment). Take the customer's name and signature and select **Picked up**.

## Afterwards

The signature, photo and notes are saved on the shipment, so the counter can answer "it never showed up" calls in seconds. Pallet deposits for the load are on the account. See [Pallet and container deposits](/help/money/pallet-deposits).
