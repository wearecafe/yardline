Confirmed orders that still have something to ship wait in the pick queue. Open **Orders and picking** to see it. Yard crew who've turned on [alerts](/help/getting-started/app-and-alerts) get one on their phone or tablet when a new order lands in the queue at their yards.

## Work the queue

1. Select an order to see its pick list: each item with its quantity, bin and colour run, the total **Load weight**, any **Deposits**, the **Delivery** details and the **Payment** status.
2. Choose a **Picker**, or leave it as **Assign picker later**.
3. Select **Start picking**. The order now has a pick list in progress.
4. Select **Open on picker** to work it on a tablet or phone, or **Print pick ticket** for a paper copy.

Orders that have already shipped some items are marked **Part shipped**.

## Shipping part of an order

When you start picking you can lower the quantities to ship only part of the order now, for example when the rest isn't in yet. Whatever isn't picked stays open on the order for the next pick list.

## Releasing everything in one step

On a confirmed order you can skip the kiosk: **Hand over all** (for a pickup) or **Load all** (for a delivery) picks and loads everything left on the order at once. Use it for small orders the counter hands over itself.

An order paid up front can't be released until it's paid.
