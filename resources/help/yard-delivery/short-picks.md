Sometimes the yard is short: only four pallets left in a run, or the screenings pile is lower than it looked.

## Record a short pick

1. On the kiosk, select **Short or substitute** on the line.
2. Enter what you actually loaded.
3. Add a **Note**: what's short, or the substitute you're sending, for example "Only 4 pallets of run 2611-B".
4. Select **Record short**.

A line also counts as short if what you loaded is more than 5% under the order.

## What happens next

- The load goes out with what was picked. Stock comes off by the actual quantity.
- The rest stays open on the order. The order shows **Part shipped** in the queue.
- Start another pick list for the rest when it's in. Each load is its own shipment with its own proof of delivery.

> **Tip** Call the customer before the truck leaves if a short pick changes their job. The note on the line tells the counter what happened.
