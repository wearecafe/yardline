A quote is a draft order sent to the customer. Prices hold until the quote expires.

## Send a quote

1. [Create an order](/help/selling/create-an-order) as a draft and add the items.
2. Set **Good until**, the date the prices hold to.
3. Leave **Email it to** ticked to email the contact a link to their quote page, and select **Send quote**.

The quote page shows the items, delivery, tax, any refundable deposits and the total. Use **Customer's quote page** on the order to see exactly what they see, or **Copy link** to send it another way.

## Changing a quote

Edit the draft and select **Send revised quote**. Each send after a change is a new revision, and YardRunner keeps a copy of what the customer was shown each time.

## When the customer accepts

The customer enters their name, and a PO number if they have one, and selects **Accept quote**. The order shows **Accepted** with their name.

Accepting doesn't confirm the order: you still [confirm it](/help/selling/confirming-orders), which runs the credit check and issues the invoice. A customer can't accept a quote after it expires.

> **Note** A quote doesn't hold stock. Stock is committed when the order is confirmed.
