The **Counter** screen is for sales that leave with the customer: find the items, take payment and hand them over in one go.

## Ring up a sale

1. Open **Counter** and check the **Yard** at the top is the one you're standing in.
2. Leave the customer as a walk-in, or search for an account. Pick a **Contact** if you know who's buying.
3. Tap items to add them, or search by name or SKU. Filter by **Category** to browse.
4. On each line, choose the **Unit** (for example bag or pallet) and use **More** and **Less** to set the quantity. Prices follow the account's price list and contract prices automatically.
5. Add a **PO number** if the account uses them.
6. Choose how it's paid and select **Pay now**.

Container and pallet deposits are added at checkout. When the sale is done, print the receipt. The stock comes off right away.

## Taking payment

| Customer | How they can pay                                          |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Walk-in  | Card on the terminal, cash, cheque or e-Transfer          |
| Account  | The same, or **on account** if the sale fits their credit |

For cash, enter the **Cash tendered** and Yardline shows the change. For a card terminal or cheque, enter the auth or cheque number as the **Reference**.

If an account sale goes over the credit limit, or the account is on credit hold, it has to be paid now unless an owner or admin overrides it. See [Credit limits and holds](/help/accounts/credit-limits).

> **Tip** The counter is for pickups. For anything going out on a truck, or that needs picking in the yard, start an order instead. See [Creating an order](/help/selling/create-an-order).
