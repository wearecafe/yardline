Contractors can reorder from the [contractor portal](/help/for-customers/contractor-portal). Their order doesn't go straight to the yard: it arrives as a **draft** marked as coming from the portal.

## Handling a portal order

1. Find it under **Orders and picking → All orders**, or on **Today**.
2. Check the items, quantities, yard and delivery details. The contractor's prices are already applied.
3. Call or email the contractor if anything needs changing.
4. [Confirm it](/help/selling/confirming-orders). It then goes to the pick queue like any other order.

> **Tip** Contractors see their reorder as waiting for the yard to confirm, so confirm portal orders promptly.
