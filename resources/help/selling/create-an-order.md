Use an order when material needs picking, goes out for delivery, or when you want to quote it first.

## Start the order

1. Select **New order** on **Today**, or go to **Orders and picking → All orders**.
2. Choose the **Organization** and **Contact**, or leave them blank for a walk-in.
3. Pick the **Yard** it ships from.
4. Choose **Pickup at yard** or **Delivery**. For a delivery, pick a **Job site** to fill in the address, or type one in.
5. Set the **Wanted on** date, a **PO number** if the account uses them, and any **Notes** for the yard or driver (gate codes, loading instructions).
6. Select **Start order**.

## Add items

Type a name or SKU under **Add item**, choose the unit and quantity, and select **Add**. Each line is priced for that account automatically. See [Which price applies](/help/products-pricing/which-price-applies).

People with **Set manual line prices** (owners and admins by default) can type over a line's **Price**. Sales can't, unless they're given it.

## Delivery details

The delivery fee is worked out from your delivery settings when you save:

- **By zone** uses the delivery postal code.
- **By distance** needs the **Distance from the yard (km)**.
- **By truckload** uses the weight of the order.

You can type over the **Delivery fee** on any order. Tick **Needs a boom truck** so dispatch puts it on the right truck. See [Delivery fees](/help/yard-delivery/delivery-fees).

## What next

An order stays a **draft** until you confirm it. You can change anything on a draft, send it as a [quote](/help/selling/quotes), or [confirm it](/help/selling/confirming-orders).

If you change the organization on a draft, its items are re-priced for the new account.
