---
title: Transferring stock between yards
summary: Move stock from one yard to another.
order: 4
related: getting-started/yards, inventory/colour-runs
---

1. On **Stock**, select **Move stock between yards**.
2. **Pick a product** and enter the quantity.
3. Choose the yard it's coming **From** and going **To**.
4. Select **Move stock**.

Stock leaves one yard and arrives at the other in the same step. Colour-run products stay in the same run, so the receiving yard knows what it has.
