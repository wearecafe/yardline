## Add your suppliers

On **Purchasing**, under **Suppliers**, add each supplier with an email, an **Orders email** if they take orders at a different address, and their **Lead time in days**. Set **Buy from** on each product to its usual supplier.

## Reorder what's low

**Purchasing** lists products **Below reorder point** with what's on hand and on order. Tick the ones to reorder, choose the supplier and the yard to **Deliver to**, and select **New purchase order**. You can also start an empty purchase order.

## Fill in and send the purchase order

1. Add lines with the product, **Quantity** and **Landed cost**.
2. Set the **Expected** date and any **Notes for the supplier**, for example "Deliver to the north gate. Call 30 min out."
3. Select **Save purchase order**.
4. Select **Email to** the supplier. Without a supplier email, select **Mark as sent** and send it your own way.

## Receive a delivery

When the truck arrives, open the purchase order and select **Receive delivery**:

1. In **Now**, enter what actually came in on each line.
2. For colour-run products, enter the **Colour run** from the tag.
3. Correct the **Landed cost** if the invoice differs.

Stock goes up right away. Anything not yet received stays open on the purchase order for the next delivery.

## Cost changes

If the landed cost is different from the product's current cost, the product's cost is updated and the product page shows a notice: how much the supplier raised or cut the cost, and your new margin. Review your [price lists](/help/products-pricing/price-lists) when you see it.
