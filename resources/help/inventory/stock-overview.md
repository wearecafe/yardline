**Stock** shows every product you track at every yard.

| Column           | Meaning                                             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| On hand          | What's physically in the yard                       |
| Committed        | On confirmed orders that haven't shipped            |
| Available        | On hand less committed: what you can still sell     |
| Lot / colour run | For products tracked by colour run, one row per run |

Filter by yard, bin, category, or **Below reorder point**. The cards at the top show products below their reorder point, pallets out on deposit, purchase orders arriving this week, count variance over the last 30 days, and counts in progress.

## Every change is a movement

Stock never changes without a record. Receiving, sales, returns, transfers, counts, adjustments and rentals going out and coming back each write a movement with who did it and when. The latest are listed under **Recent movements** on **Stock**.

## Reorder points

Set **Reorder at** in **Update stock** (it applies to the product) to flag it when stock drops below that level. Products below their reorder point are listed on **Purchasing** ready to reorder. See [Purchase orders and receiving](/help/inventory/purchase-orders).
