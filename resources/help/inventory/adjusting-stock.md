---
title: Adjusting stock
summary: Receive without a PO, set a count, write off damage or adjust by any amount.
order: 2
related: inventory/cycle-counts, inventory/stock-overview
---

On **Stock**, select a row and choose **Update stock**. There are four kinds of change:

| Choose  | Use it for                                               |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Receive | Stock that came in without a purchase order              |
| Count   | Setting on-hand to what you counted                      |
| Damaged | Removing broken or lost stock                            |
| Adjust  | Adding or removing by any amount (use a minus to remove) |

Add a **Note**, like a slip number or reason, so the movement makes sense later. For colour-run products, choose or enter the **Colour run**.

> **Tip** For counting a whole section of the yard, use a [cycle count](/help/inventory/cycle-counts) instead. It handles many products at once and shows the differences before posting.
