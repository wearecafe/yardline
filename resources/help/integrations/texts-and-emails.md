YardRunner sends messages under your business name. Set a **Reply-to email** under **Settings → Business** so replies come to you.

Emails carry your logo and colours (see [Your logo and colours on emails](/help/integrations/email-branding)), and can come from your own addresses once you connect your domain (see [Sending from your own domain](/help/integrations/sending-domain)).

## Emails

| Email                   | Sent to             | When                                              |
| ----------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Quote                   | The order's contact | When you send a quote with **Email it to** ticked |
| Statement               | The account         | When you email a statement                        |
| Purchase order          | The supplier        | When you select **Email to** on a purchase order  |
| Signed rental agreement | The renter          | After they sign                                   |
| Rental receipt          | The renter          | At check-in                                       |
| Portal sign-in link     | The contact         | When they ask for one at the portal               |

## Texts

| Text             | Sent to             | When                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Delivery window  | The order's contact | When dispatch selects **Send ETA texts**                                                   |
| Signing link     | The renter          | When you select **Text signing link to customer**                                          |
| Rental reminders | The renter          | Day before pickup, before return, and daily while overdue, if **Text reminders** is ticked |

Texts go to the customer's phone number, so keep mobile numbers up to date on **Customers**.
