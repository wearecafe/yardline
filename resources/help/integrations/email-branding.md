Emails to your customers and suppliers carry your brand: quotes, statements, rental emails, purchase orders and portal sign-in links. An owner or admin sets it up under **Settings → Branding**. Changes show in the preview beside the form as you make them.

Emails to your own staff (invites, password resets, time corrections) come from YardRunner and look like YardRunner.

## What you can set

| Setting                  | What it does                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Logo                     | Shown at the top of every email. Use a PNG or JPG at least 40 pixels tall, up to 2 MB. Without a logo, your business name is shown |
| Primary colour           | The colour bar, buttons and accents. Type a hex code or pick one of the suggestions                                                |
| Header style             | **Light** puts your logo on white under a colour bar. **Brand** puts it on your primary colour                                     |
| Tax registration numbers | Shown with your business name and address at the bottom of each email, for example your HST number                                 |
| Footer note              | An optional line under every email, like a slogan or your terms                                                                    |

From your primary colour, YardRunner works out lighter and darker shades for links, the key facts box and the text on buttons, so everything stays easy to read whatever colour you choose.

Press **Save branding**, then **Send me a test email** to see a sample in your own inbox.

## Changing individual colours

If you need to match exact brand colours, ask YardRunner support to turn on the **colour system**. You then see every colour YardRunner made from your primary, and can type a different hex code for any of them. Changed colours are marked **Custom** and stay as they are if you change your primary later; **Reset** puts one back.

Readability checks under the colours show whether text and buttons are easy to read. You can't save colours that fail a required check.

## Where emails come from

Until you connect your own domain, customer emails come from a YardRunner address with your business name, and replies go to the reply-to address under **Settings → Business**. To send from your own addresses, see [Sending from your own domain](/help/integrations/sending-domain).
