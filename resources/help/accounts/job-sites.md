A job site is a delivery address an account uses again and again, like a subdivision lot or a contractor's shop.

## Add a job site

On the organization's page, under **Job sites**, select **Add site** and enter:

- **Site name**, for example "Lot 14, Mountainview subdivision"
- **Usual yard**, the yard that normally supplies it
- The address and postal code. The postal code sets the delivery zone.
- **Delivery notes**: shown to the driver. Gate codes, where to drop, who to call.
- **PO number required**: every order to this site needs a PO number before it can be confirmed.
- **Default site**: picked automatically on new orders for this account.

Mark a finished job **Inactive** to keep it off new orders without losing its history.
