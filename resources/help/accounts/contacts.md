Customers are people. A contact can buy for one or more organizations, or for none (a walk-in or homeowner who rents equipment).

## Add a contact to an organization

On the organization's page, under **Contacts**:

- **New contact** creates a person and adds them to this account.
- **Link existing** adds someone already in YardRunner, for example a foreman who works for two contractors.

For each contact, choose a **Role** and whether they **Can charge to the account**.

| Role          | Used for                           |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Buyer         | Places orders                      |
| Approver      | Places and approves orders         |
| Account admin | Looks after the account's contacts |

The role tells your staff who they're dealing with. What decides whether an order can go on account is **Can charge to the account**: if it's off, that person's orders must be paid up front.

## Why contacts matter

- **Email** is where quotes, statements and portal sign-in links go.
- **Phone** is where delivery ETA texts and rental reminders go.
- Any contact with an email can sign in to the [contractor portal](/help/for-customers/contractor-portal).

You can also manage people from **Customers**, including walk-ins and renters, and see which accounts each one **Buys for**.
