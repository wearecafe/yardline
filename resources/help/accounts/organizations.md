Contractors, builders and other businesses that buy from you are **organizations**. An organization holds the account: its price list, terms, credit limit, job sites and the people allowed to buy for it.

## Add an organization

1. Go to **Organizations** and select **Add organization**.
2. Under **Account**, enter the name, an account number if you use them, and an email and phone.
3. Enter the **Billing address**. It's printed on invoices and statements.
4. Under **Pricing and credit**, choose:
    - **Price list**: leave blank for list price. See [Price lists](/help/products-pricing/price-lists).
    - **Credit limit**: how much the account can owe at once. With no limit, orders must be paid up front.
    - **Terms**: Due on receipt, Net 15, Net 30 or Net 60.
5. If the account doesn't pay sales tax, tick **Tax exempt** and enter the **Exemption number**.
6. Add any **Notes** for staff and save.

Only owners and admins can set the credit limit, terms and credit hold.

## The organization page

The organization's page shows its orders and balances, **Credit used** against its limit, its [contacts](/help/accounts/contacts), [job sites](/help/accounts/job-sites) and [contract prices](/help/products-pricing/contract-prices), the pallets it's holding, and a **History** of changes. Select **Statement** to print or email a statement, or **New order** to start an order for it.
