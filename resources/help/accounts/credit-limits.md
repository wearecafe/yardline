Every organization has a **credit limit** and **terms**. Orders go on account only while the account has credit available.

## Available credit

Available credit is the credit limit less the unpaid balance of the account's confirmed and fulfilled orders on account. You'll see it on the organization's page as **Credit used**, and on every draft as **Available credit**.

## When an order goes over

If an order would take the account over its limit, it must be paid up front. An owner or admin can override instead:

1. Select **Override credit limit** on the order.
2. Enter a **Reason for override**.
3. Select **Confirm on account anyway**.

The reason is saved on the order with their name, and appears in the [audit log](/help/getting-started/audit-log).

## Credit hold

Tick **Credit hold** on an organization to stop new orders going on account, for example while invoices are overdue. Until the hold is lifted, new orders must be paid up front. The account can still buy, and an owner or admin can still override.

## No credit limit

An account with a credit limit of zero has no credit: every order is paid up front unless an owner or admin overrides.

> **Tip** Check **Receivables** each week for accounts that are overdue, and use credit holds rather than turning customers away at the counter. See [Receivables and statements](/help/money/receivables-and-statements).
