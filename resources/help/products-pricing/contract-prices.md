A contract price is a price agreed with one organization for one product. It beats their price list while it's in effect.

## Add a contract price

1. Open the organization and find **Contract prices**.
2. Select **Add**, then **Pick a product** and the **Unit**.
3. Enter the **Price**.
4. Set **From** and **Until** dates if it's for a particular job or season.

To see every account with its own price on a product, open the product and look under **Customer prices**.

> **Note** Contract prices are part of the [audit log](/help/getting-started/audit-log), so you can always see who agreed a price and when.
