YardRunner works out the price on every line for that account, unit, quantity and date. The first of these that applies wins:

1. **Contract price**: a price agreed with the organization, in effect that day.
2. **Price list**: the organization's price list. Of the prices in effect that day, the one with the highest **From quantity** the order reaches.
3. **Retail price**: the selling unit's retail price.

Walk-ins always get the retail price.

## Prices for other units

A contract or price list entry is for one unit. An entry for the base unit also covers the other selling units, converted by how many base units each is worth. For example, a contract price of $50 per tonne gives $25 for a half-tonne unit.

## Changing a price by hand

Owners and admins can type a different price on an order line. That line then keeps its price when the order is re-priced. Staff can't change line prices.

## When prices are re-checked

Draft lines are re-priced when the order changes, for example when you change the organization or the quantity. Once an order is confirmed, its prices are fixed. A [quote](/help/selling/quotes) holds its prices until it expires.
