A price list is a set of trade prices, for example Contractor or Builder. Give an organization a price list and its orders use those prices instead of list price.

## Create a price list

1. Go to **Price lists** and select **Create price list**.
2. Name it (for example Contractor) and add a description.

## Add prices

On the price list, use **Add or change a price**:

1. **Choose a product** and the **Unit** the price is for.
2. Enter the **From quantity**. Leave it at 1 for the normal price.
3. Enter the **Price**.
4. Optionally set **From** and **Until** dates. Leave them blank for a price that applies from now on.
5. Select **Save price**.

Products not on the list sell at list price.

## Quantity breaks

Add more than one price for the same product and unit with different **From quantity** values. Each price applies from its minimum quantity up. For example:

| From qty | Price per pallet |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| 1        | $420.00          |
| 10       | $395.00          |
| 25       | $380.00          |

An order for 12 pallets gets $395.00.

## Give a price list to an account

Edit the organization and choose its **Price list** under **Pricing and credit**. Blank means list price. You can also set prices on the selling units grid on each product.

> **Tip** Use dated prices to load a supplier's spring increase ahead of time. The new prices take over on their start date.
