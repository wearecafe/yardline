## Pallet deposits

### Set up

1. Under **Settings → Delivery → Returns and pallet deposits**, tick **Charge pallet deposits** and set the **Pallet deposit** amount.
2. On each product that ships on pallets, tick **Ships on pallets that carry a deposit** and enter the **Base units per pallet**.

### Pallets going out

Pallets are counted when a load leaves the yard, from what was actually picked, and the deposit goes on the order. Each account's page shows how many pallets it's holding, and **Stock** and **Receivables** show the total out on deposit.

### Pallets coming back

On **Returns**, use **Pallets back**: pick the account (or walk-in), the number of pallets and the yard, and select **Credit pallets**. The deposit is credited back to the account at the deposit rate.

Contractors can ask you to collect pallets from a job site with **Book a pallet return pickup** in the [contractor portal](/help/for-customers/contractor-portal). The pickup lands on the [dispatch board](/help/yard-delivery/dispatch-board).

## Container deposits

For things like bulk bags, set a **Container deposit** on the selling unit. It's added to every order for that unit and shown to the customer as a refundable deposit. When the container comes back, take a return on the order to credit it. See [Returns and credit notes](/help/money/returns-and-credit-notes).

## Deposits on a product

A product can also carry a **Deposit** that's taken up front per unit. Use this for items where the deposit isn't tied to a selling unit.
