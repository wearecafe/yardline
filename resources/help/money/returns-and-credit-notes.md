## Take a return

1. Open the original order and select **Take a return**.
2. For each item coming back, enter the quantity **Coming back** and its **Condition**:
    - **Back on the shelf**: goes back into stock, less the restocking fee.
    - **Damaged, write off**: credited, but not put back into stock.
3. Choose the yard it goes **Back into**.
4. Check the **Restocking fee (%)**. It applies to goods going back on the shelf. Your default is set under **Settings → Delivery → Returns and pallet deposits**.
5. Enter a **Reason**, for example "Over-ordered, 3 pallets unopened".
6. Select **Issue credit note**.

The credit includes the goods, less the restocking fee, plus their share of the original tax, and any container deposits on what came back.

Your return window (30 days unless you change it) is set in the same place.

## Use the credit

A credit note sits on the account until it's used. Open it from **Returns** and:

- **Apply credit** to one of the account's orders or invoices, or
- **Record refund** if you gave the money back, choosing how (cash, cheque, e-Transfer or card) and the amount.

Unused credits show on [receivables](/help/money/receivables-and-statements) and reduce what the account owes.
