**Receivables** shows open invoices on account, by how late they are, less any unused credits.

## Aging

Each account with a balance is listed with what it owes in each bucket:

| Bucket  | Meaning                    |
| ------- | -------------------------- |
| Current | Not yet due                |
| 1–30    | Up to 30 days past due     |
| 31–60   | 31 to 60 days past due     |
| 61–90   | 61 to 90 days past due     |
| 90+     | More than 90 days past due |

It also shows unused credits and the net owing. Accounts on credit hold are marked **On hold**. The totals at the top show what's **Owing on account**, what's **Overdue**, **Unused credits** and **Pallets out on deposit**.

## Statements

Select **Statement** on an account (here or on the organization page). A statement lists the account's open invoices with their age, recent payments and credits, and what's owed now. You can:

- print it,
- download it as a PDF, or
- email it to the account.

Contractors can also see their statement any time in the [contractor portal](/help/for-customers/contractor-portal).

> **Tip** Run statements at month-end, then put accounts that are well past due on [credit hold](/help/accounts/credit-limits).
