Every confirmed goods order gets an invoice, issued at the moment it's confirmed.

| Order         | Invoice is due                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| On account    | By the account's terms: on receipt, Net 15, Net 30 or Net 60 |
| Paid up front | On receipt                                                   |

The invoice carries the PO number, the delivery address, the lines, deposits, delivery fee and tax. Open it from the order to print it.

Payments recorded on the order are applied to its invoice. Open invoices on account show up in [receivables](/help/money/receivables-and-statements) until they're paid.

Rentals are billed separately from goods. See [Rental deposits and payments](/help/rentals/deposits-and-payments).
