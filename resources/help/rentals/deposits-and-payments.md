Equipment can't go out until the rental is paid in full and the deposit is held.

## Ways to pay

| Option                        | How it works                                                                       |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Charge card on file at pickup | The customer's saved card is charged when the equipment goes out                   |
| Charge card on file now       | Charge it straight away                                                            |
| Send a payment link           | The customer pays by card from a link, which also saves their card for the deposit |
| Pay at the counter            | Card on your terminal, cash, cheque or e-Transfer                                  |

To take a counter payment, open the rental and select **Record counter payment**. To send a link, use **Open payment page** or **Copy link**.

## The deposit

The deposit is the total of each unit's deposit. On a saved card it's held, not charged: the money is reserved on the card until the rental closes. You can also take a deposit on the terminal, in cash or by cheque at check-out.

> **Heads up** Card holds usually last about seven days. For longer rentals, take the deposit another way or talk to us about your options.

## When it comes back

At check-in, extra charges (fuel, cleaning, damage, late return) are taken from the deposit, and the rest is released. See [Check-out and check-in](/help/rentals/check-out-and-check-in).

## Rental payments

**Rental payments** shows what's been **Taken this month**, **Deposits on hold**, and what's **Owing**, with every payment, deposit hold, deposit kept, deposit released and refund.
