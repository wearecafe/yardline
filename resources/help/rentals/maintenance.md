**Maintenance** shows units **Due for service**, units **In the shop**, and **Recent work**.

## Service intervals

Each unit has a service interval in hours, days, or both. When a unit passes it, it's shown as **Overdue · locked out** and can't be booked until the service is logged.

## Log work

On **Maintenance**, use **Log work** (or **Log service** on a unit's page) and choose the **Type**:

| Type                              | What happens                                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Service done (resets the counter) | Logs a completed service with its hours and cost, and resets the service counter |
| Send to the shop for service      | Opens a shop ticket. The unit is **In the shop** and can't be booked.            |
| Send to the shop for repair       | The same, for a repair                                                           |

Describe **What was done** (for example "250-hr service, oil and filters"), the **Hour meter** and the **Cost**.

When shop work is finished, select **Mark done** and enter the cost. Once a unit has no open shop work it goes **Back in fleet**.

Maintenance cost is shown on the unit page against its rental revenue.
