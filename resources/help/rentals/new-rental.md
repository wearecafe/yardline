Select **New rental** on **Today**, **Fleet** or **Rentals and contracts**.

## Fill it in

1. **Customer**: search by name, company or phone. Customers are shared with your yard accounts. Choose **Add a new customer** for someone new. The page shows whether their **Driver's licence** and insurance certificate are on file.
2. **Equipment and dates**: choose the equipment and when it goes **Out** and comes **Back**. A free unit is picked for you; choose **Add another unit** for more.
3. **Yard**: where it goes out from.
4. **Pickup or delivery**: **Customer picks up**, or **We deliver and pick up**. For delivery, enter the address; the delivery zone comes from the postal code.
5. **Protection and charges on return**:
    - Offer the **Damage waiver**. Customers can decline it.
    - Tick **Bill fuel and cleaning on return if needed**.
    - Tick **Text reminders** to text the customer the day before it's due back and if it's overdue.
6. Add **Notes**: site access, the operator, anything the yard should know.

The **Summary** shows the price as you go: the rental charge, damage waiver, delivery, tax and the deposit.

## Save it

| Choose                        | When                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Save quote                    | The customer is still deciding. Nothing is held.            |
| Create agreement for signing  | They're at the counter. Opens the agreement on this device. |
| Text signing link to customer | They'll sign and pay from their phone.                      |
| Reserve without signing       | Hold the units now and sign at pickup.                      |

Choose how it will be paid: charge the card on file at pickup or now, send a payment link, or pay at the counter. See [Rental deposits and payments](/help/rentals/deposits-and-payments).
