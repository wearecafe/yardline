**Calendar** shows each unit as a row and each day as a column, a week at a time. Use **Earlier week** and **Later week** to move around.

A unit is unavailable when it's:

- reserved or on rent for another rental,
- in the shop, or
- due for service (locked out until the service is logged).

Yardline never lets two rentals book the same unit at the same time.

## Moving a booking

Select a booking and choose **Swap to another unit** to move it to a free unit of the same equipment, for example when the original unit goes into the shop.
