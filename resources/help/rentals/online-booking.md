Your yard has a public booking page where customers can see your equipment, check availability, and book and pay.

## Put equipment on it

On each piece of rental equipment, tick **Bookable online** and add a one-line description. Only equipment that's bookable online appears.

## How online bookings work

- Customers pick the equipment and the days. Bookings are for whole days: out at opening time, back by closing time on the return day.
- Online bookings are pickups from your default yard.
- The customer enters their name, email and mobile number, chooses whether to take the damage waiver, and pays by card. Their card is saved for the deposit.
- They then sign the agreement on their phone.
- The rental shows on **Rentals and contracts** marked **Booked online**, already reserved.

Returning customers are matched by email.

## Linking to it

Your booking page is at `/book/` followed by your business's short name on YardRunner. Contact us if you need the exact link for your website.

> **Tip** Contractors signed in to the [contractor portal](/help/for-customers/contractor-portal) get a **Rent equipment** link straight to your booking page.
