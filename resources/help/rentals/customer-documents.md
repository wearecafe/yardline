Keep a renter's driver's licence and insurance certificate on file so you only collect them once.

## Add a document

1. Open the customer from **Customers** and find **Licence and insurance**.
2. Choose the **Document**: driver's licence or insurance certificate.
3. Add a **Photo or PDF**.
4. Enter the **Number**. The last few digits are enough.
5. Enter the **Expires** date and select **Add to file**.

The rental page shows whether a licence and insurance certificate are on file. A licence photographed while signing an agreement at the counter is saved too.

> **Note** Documents are stored privately. Only signed-in staff at your business can see them.
