Every rental needs a signed agreement before equipment goes out.

## What the customer signs

The agreement shows **What you're agreeing to**: the equipment, dates, charges, damage waiver choice, deposit and your late fee policy, followed by your **Full terms**. The terms are copied onto the agreement when it's created, so later changes to your terms never change an agreement already offered. If the rental changes before it's signed, the agreement is updated.

Set your terms under **Settings → Rentals**. See [Rental settings](/help/rentals/rental-settings).

## Signing at the counter

1. On the rental, select **Create agreement for signing** (or **Open agreement for signing**).
2. **Photograph the driver's licence**.
3. The customer enters their **Printed name**, signs in the box, and selects **Sign and continue to payment**.

## Signing from a link

Select **Text signing link to customer**. They open it on their phone, read the agreement, sign, and pay.

## After signing

The signed agreement is saved as a PDF, with the signature, time and device, and emailed to the customer. Select **View the signed agreement (PDF)** to see it.
