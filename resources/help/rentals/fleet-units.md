A unit is one machine, tracked by its serial or fleet number. Customers book the equipment; each rental gets specific units.

## Add a unit

1. Go to **Fleet** and select **Add unit**.
2. Choose the **Equipment** it belongs to, and enter its **Serial or fleet number** (for example PC-04).
3. Choose its home **Yard** and **Status**: Available, In the shop, or Retired.
4. Under **Hours and service**, enter the current **Hour meter**, the service interval (**Service every** so many hours or days) and when it was last serviced.
5. Optionally enter when you bought it and its **Cost**, so the unit page can show revenue against cost.

## The fleet list

**Fleet** shows every unit with its status, who has it and when it's due back, its hours, its next service and its day rate. The cards at the top show utilization over the last 30 days, what's **Due back today**, what's **Overdue**, and units with **Service due within 10 hrs**.

## The unit page

Open a unit to see its **Rental history**, **Rental revenue**, **Maintenance cost**, **Next service** and **Maintenance log**, and to log service. See [Maintenance and lockouts](/help/rentals/maintenance).
