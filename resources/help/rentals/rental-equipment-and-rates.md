Rental equipment is set up in two parts: the **equipment** (for example "Plate compactor, 200 lb") with its rates, and the individual **units** in your fleet. Start with the equipment.

## Add the equipment

1. Go to **Products**, select **Add product** and choose **Rental equipment**.
2. Enter the SKU, name and category, and the **Day rate**.
3. Under **Rental rates and fleet**, fill in what applies:

| Field                         | What it does                                                                             |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hourly, Half day, Week, Month | Extra rates. A half day is 4 hours, a month is 4 weeks. Leave any blank you don't offer. |
| Weekend                       | A flat rate for Friday or Saturday pickup, returned by Monday noon                       |
| Minimum charge                | The least a rental can cost                                                              |
| Deposit                       | Held on the customer's card for each unit, released when it comes back                   |
| Fuel charge                   | Charged if it comes back not full (half the charge if it's half full)                    |
| Cleaning fee                  | Charged if it comes back dirty                                                           |
| Service every (hours / days)  | The default service interval for units of this equipment                                 |

4. To offer it on your public booking page, tick **Bookable online** and add a one-line description, for example "Compacting base for patios and walkways".

Then add the units. See [Adding units to the fleet](/help/rentals/fleet-units).

## How a rental is priced

Yardline charges the cheapest combination of your rates that covers the time out:

- Time out is rounded up to whole hours, with a 30-minute grace, so a 9:00 to 9:20 next-day rental is one day.
- Any mix of rates can be used, so a week replaces seven days whenever it's cheaper.
- The weekend rate applies when it's cheaper than the normal rates.
- Sunday is free when a rental starts on a Saturday and runs through Sunday, if you've turned that on.
- A rental never costs less than the minimum charge.

Damage waiver, delivery and tax are added on top. See [Rental settings](/help/rentals/rental-settings).
