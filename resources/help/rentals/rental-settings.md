Owners and admins set the rental policy under **Settings → Rentals**.

| Setting                        | What it does                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Damage waiver (%)              | The waiver's price, as a percentage of the rental charge. Customers can decline it.        |
| Late grace (minutes)           | How late a return can be before late charges start                                         |
| Opens and Closes               | Your rental counter hours. Online bookings go out at opening and are due back by closing.  |
| Return reminder (hours before) | When the return reminder text goes out                                                     |
| Free Sunday                    | Sunday is free when picked up Saturday and returned Monday morning                         |
| Late fee policy                | A sentence shown on the agreement, for example "Each late day is charged at the day rate." |
| Rental terms                   | The terms customers sign. Have them reviewed by your lawyer.                               |

Delivery zones used for rental drop-offs are set under **Settings → Delivery**. See [Delivery fees and zones](/help/yard-delivery/delivery-fees).

## Reminder texts

When **Text reminders** is ticked on a rental, the customer gets:

- a pickup reminder the day before,
- a return reminder at the time you set, and
- an overdue notice once a day while it's late.

Whatever the rental's **Text reminders** setting, the rental desk at its yard also gets an overdue [alert](/help/getting-started/app-and-alerts) once a day while it's late, on any device where they've turned alerts on.
