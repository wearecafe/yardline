Equipment rentals run alongside your yard sales, with the same customers and accounts. Every rental is prepaid, with a deposit held until the equipment comes back.

## The life of a rental

| Status    | What's happened                                                  |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quote     | Equipment and dates priced. Nothing is held.                     |
| Reserved  | The units are booked for those dates and can't be double-booked. |
| On rent   | Signed, paid, deposit held, inspected and out the gate.          |
| Returned  | Checked in, extra charges settled, deposit released.             |
| Cancelled | Stopped before it went out.                                      |

The list on **Rentals and contracts** also flags rentals that are **Overdue**, **Unsigned**, or **Booked online**.

## Step by step

1. **Quote or reserve** at the counter or on the phone. See [Creating a rental](/help/rentals/new-rental). Customers can also [book online](/help/rentals/online-booking).
2. **Sign the agreement**, on the counter tablet or from a link texted to the customer. See [Agreements and e-signing](/help/rentals/agreements-and-signing).
3. **Take payment and the deposit.** See [Rental deposits and payments](/help/rentals/deposits-and-payments).
4. **Check out**: photos, hour meter, fuel and condition for each unit.
5. **Check in** when it comes back: compare, add any fuel, cleaning, damage or late charges, and settle the deposit. See [Check-out and check-in](/help/rentals/check-out-and-check-in).

Delivered rentals put a drop-off and a pickup on the [dispatch board](/help/yard-delivery/dispatch-board).

Units that are due for service are locked out of new rentals until the service is logged. See [Maintenance and lockouts](/help/rentals/maintenance).
