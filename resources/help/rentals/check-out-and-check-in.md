## Check-out

A rental can go out once it's reserved, the agreement is signed, it's paid in full and the deposit is held.

1. Open the rental and select **Check out equipment**.
2. For each unit, take photos (use **Add photo** and a **Photo label**, like "Left side").
3. Under **Readings**, enter the hour meter and the **Fuel level**, and whether it's **Clean**.
4. Note any existing damage under **Condition notes**, for example "Base plate mark".
5. Confirm. The rental is now **On rent**.

## Check-in

1. Open the rental and select **Check in**.
2. Take return photos and enter the hour meter, fuel level and whether it's clean.
3. For any new damage, select **New mark**, describe it, and enter the **Damage amount**. If the customer took the damage waiver, choose whether it's **Covered by waiver**; otherwise the customer pays.
4. YardRunner adds **Charges to add**:
    - **Refuel to full**, if fuel and cleaning are billed and it isn't full
    - **Cleaning**, if it isn't clean
    - **Late return**, if it's back after your grace period, for the extra time at the best rate
5. Confirm. The charges are taken from the deposit, the rest is released, and the customer is sent a receipt.

If the unit's service is now due, it's locked out of new rentals until the service is logged. See [Maintenance and lockouts](/help/rentals/maintenance).
